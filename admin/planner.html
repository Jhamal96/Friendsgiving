What’s new

Recent organizer updates:

  • NEW: Original guest cards are back, with the six-column spreadsheet quick-edit view available as a collapsible dropdown. Both use the same Guest List records and refresh from the cloud.
  • NEW: Squad teams follow the Guest List rather than requiring Form responses. Paid and RSVP-confirmed guests join automatically; unpaid people stay off the playing teams.
  • NEW: In Guests & Payments → Not RSVP’d, use Remove Entry to archive unwanted names. Removed shows archived entries and allows restoration; Google Sheets remains unchanged.
  • NEW: Search across Guest List, payments, RSVP, games, groceries, schedules and all planner tabs; tap a result to open it.
  • NEW: In Team Assignments, choose Playing, Not Playing, Referee, Scorekeeper, Timekeeper, Coach, or a custom staff position. Non-playing people stay off team rosters and out of randomization.
  • NEW: Add emojis with the 😊 button in the admin header or next to an editable event, planning, or team motto field. Emojis are inserted at your cursor; autosave still works.
  • Event settings, guest details, payments and planner changes now save automatically after you edit.
  • Google Sheets and Form Responses connection status now live under the Settings gear. Form matching is available in the admin-only Form Responses page.
  • All organizer pages now adapt to phones, tablets, desktop screens and device rotation.
  • After you acknowledge welcome and update popups, the intro video plays with a Halo-style transition.
  • Use Page Settings to show, hide, and reorder the friends’ page sections.
  • The Squad Planner has guest payment filters, a paid total, and completion buttons.
  • Under Guests & Payments, switch between All Guests and Not RSVP’d to review unconfirmed guests, confirm RSVPs, and autosave changes.
  • The organizer menu now links to Team Assignments.
  • Google Sheets guest, RSVP, and payment synchronization is ready for a one-time Google authorization.
