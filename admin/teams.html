ⓘ Team rules & eligibility⌄

The Google Guest List is the source of truth for team players. A person is eligible only when their own Guest List entry has RSVP = Yes and Paid = Yes. An asterisk (*) on the Guest List does not affect eligibility and is omitted from team names. Unnamed payment placeholders are not players. Guests who RSVP but have not paid remain off the teams until payment is confirmed.

Every eligible named guest can be assigned independently; the Google Form is used for response review, not to decide who can play. Captains stay locked in their captain positions, even if their Guest List name has an asterisk, and are never added a second time as players.

Private drafts autosave. Choose Publish Teams to show changes to guests or Unpublish Teams to hide them without losing your draft.

Teams settings

Team eligibility comes exclusively from the current Guest List, with RSVP = Yes and Paid = Yes on the individual's own entry. An asterisk (*) does not prevent selection and will not be shown in team names. Unnamed +1 payment rows and entries removed from the planner are excluded. Form submission is not required. Named plus-ones with their own confirmed Guest List entry can be randomized independently. Players marked Not Playing or assigned staff roles are excluded. Captains keep their reserved leadership positions, even when marked * on the Guest List, and cannot appear twice. As RSVPs and payments change, the team rosters reconcile automatically. Draft changes autosave privately. Publish Teams makes them visible to guests; Unpublish Teams hides them without losing the draft.
