Teams settings

Each captain is reserved on their own team and counts as one player. Team members use plain names. Named companions from a Yes response appear as Name +1 and stay on that participant's team. Only paid and RSVP-confirmed participants are eligible. Status changes automatically add or remove eligible players and balance teams without duplicates. Team drafts autosave privately. Publish Teams makes them visible to guests; Unpublish Teams hides them immediately, without losing the draft or automatically republishing.

What’s new

Friendsgiving organizer updates:

  • Publish and Unpublish controls now let organizers show or hide team assignments without deleting private drafts.
  • Google Sheets and Form Responses connection status now live under the Settings gear. Form matching is available in the admin-only Form Responses page.
  • All organizer pages now adapt to phones, tablets, desktop screens and device rotation.
  • After you acknowledge welcome and update popups, the intro video plays with a Halo-style transition.
  • Publish a public team lineup with captains, captions and assigned guests.
  • Plus-ones from Yes responses now appear as Name +1 and remain with their participant. Captains cannot move to opposing teams; payment or RSVP changes rebalance eligible players.
  • Reorder teams and edit their rosters from Squad Assignments.
  • The main page stays read-only for guests.
