Squad assignments.

The Google Guest List is the source of truth for team players. A person is eligible only when their own Guest List entry has RSVP = Yes and Paid = Yes. Entries marked with an asterisk (*) and unnamed +1 payment placeholders are not added to the player pool. Guests who RSVP but have not paid remain off the teams until payment is confirmed. Eligible named plus-ones with their own qualifying Guest List entry can be randomized independently from their inviter; the Google Form is used only for companion labels and response review, not to decide who can play. Captains stay locked in their captain positions, even if their Guest List name has an asterisk, and are never added a second time as players. Private drafts autosave. Choose Publish Teams to show changes to guests or Unpublish Teams to hide them without losing your draft.

Teams settings

Team eligibility comes exclusively from the current Guest List, with RSVP = Yes and Paid = Yes on the individual's own entry. Entries containing * are excluded from player selection, along with unnamed +1 payment rows and entries removed from the planner. Form submission is not required. Named plus-ones with their own confirmed Guest List entry can be randomized independently. Players marked Not Playing or assigned staff roles are excluded. Captains keep their reserved leadership positions, even when marked * on the Guest List, and cannot appear twice. As RSVPs and payments change, the team rosters reconcile automatically. Draft changes autosave privately. Publish Teams makes them visible to guests; Unpublish Teams hides them without losing the draft.
